Decision Ledger chat panel

Claude Code runs underneath as usual; this is what the panel shows. While Claude works, its words stream in as written. When it finishes, the reply becomes the important information: questions, permissions, decisions and files changed, with the full reply one click away. Open any state, then step through the rest from the bar at the top.

Specs: docs/ui/chat-panel.md (the screen) and docs/ui/design-system.md (tokens and components).
